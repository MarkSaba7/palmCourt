How you play

Four ways to swing

Pick whatever you have to hand. The game handles the footwork, so every control is about one thing: the timing and shape of your swing.

Best feel

Phone racket

Your phone becomes the racket handle. Its motion sensors read every swing, with no app to install.

  1. Choose Phone in the game and scan the code with your phone.
  2. Tap Start and allow motion access.
  3. Hold it tight, give yourself room, and swing.
Webcam

Your hand

Hand tracking follows your racket hand through the webcam. It runs on your computer: the picture never leaves it.

  1. Choose Hand and allow the camera.
  2. Stand about 2 m back, with a light in front of you.
  3. Swing across your body. Raise your hand to toss a serve.
Webcam

Ping-pong paddle

Hold a brightly coloured paddle to the webcam and the game locks onto its colour. Fast and steady, even in dimmer rooms.

  1. Choose Paddle and allow the camera.
  2. Hold the paddle face in the circle and lock its colour.
  3. Swing. Red or blue rubber works best.
No camera

Mouse & keys

Click or tap to swing and flick the mouse first for power. On the keyboard, Space swings, Shift+Space hits harder and S slices.

  1. Choose Mouse.
  2. Watch the Forehand / Backhand cue.
  3. Time the click as the ball arrives.

Timing aims the shot: swing early to pull it cross-court, late to send it down the line. Low-to-high adds topspin, high-to-low slices, and a faster swing hits harder (and misses more). Full guide, camera tips and troubleshooting.

What’s in the game

Real tennis, in a browser tab

Your swing, your shot

Pace, spin and direction come from how and when you swing: flat drives, heavy topspin, slices, lobs and serves you toss yourself.

A tour of rivals

Five fictional pros, each with their own game: counter-punchers, big hitters, net rushers and drop-shot artists. Pick Rookie, Club or Pro difficulty.

Play a friend online

Create a match link, send it, and play each other in real time. No sign-up, nothing to install.

Three surfaces, three lights

Hard, clay and grass courts that bounce and slide differently, played by day, at golden hour or at night under the floodlights.

Broadcast presentation

TV cameras, a live scoreboard, instant replays of the big points and a line review on the close calls, in a stadium full of fans.

Progress that sticks

Level up, earn Fuzz, unlock courts, pros and kit in the Pro Shop, and chase daily challenges, achievements and a World Tour title. Progress saves in your browser.

Questions

Before you play

Is Palm Court really free?

Yes. The browser version is free to play, with no account and no download. It shows a few ads between matches, which is what keeps it free. There are no in-app purchases: everything in the game is earned by playing.

Do I need a webcam or a phone?

No. You can play with a mouse or the keyboard. A phone (used as the racket) or a webcam (tracking your hand or a ping-pong paddle) makes it feel like real tennis.

Does the camera record me?

No. Camera frames are analysed on your own computer, in the page, and thrown away. They are never uploaded, recorded or saved. Read the privacy policy.

Which browsers work?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a computer. Chrome and Edge give the smoothest webcam tracking. The phone racket works in the phone’s own browser.

Is there multiplayer?

Yes. Create a match link, send it to a friend and play each other in real time. Neither of you needs an account.
