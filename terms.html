Play safely

Palm Court is played with real movement, and you are responsible for your own safety and your surroundings.

  • Clear the space. Make sure there is enough room to swing in every direction, with no people, pets, furniture, screens, windows, lamps or ceiling fans within reach.
  • Hold on. When you use a phone as the racket, hold it firmly, use a wrist strap or a grippy case, and don’t let go mid-swing. Dry your hands if they get sweaty.
  • Know your limits. Warm up, take regular breaks, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness or discomfort. Ask a doctor before playing if you have a medical condition, an injury or are pregnant.
